-----------------------------------------------------------
 B3. NEU CO LOI
-----------------------------------------------------------

Tool tu kiem tra may truoc khi chay va noi ro thieu gi.

  Hien dong "DeprecationWarning: Passing args to a child process..."
      -> KHONG PHAI LOI. Day la canh bao cua Node 24, vo hai.
         Cu de yen, tool van chay dung.

  Tieu de cua so CMD hien chu "Select ..." va moi thu dung im
      -> KHONG PHAI DO. Anh lo click vao trong cua so CMD nen Windows
         bat che do boi den va TAM DUNG tien trinh.
         Bam phim ESC la chay tiep ngay.
         Dung giao-dien.bat thi khong bao gio gap loi nay.

  Man hinh im lang mot luc
      -> Binh thuong. Moi video in tien do theo tung 10%.
         Lan dau chay lau hon vi phai dung bundle.

  "Chua co Node.js" / "Chua co Python"
      -> Cai theo B1, roi MO LAI cua so CMD.

  "python not found" du da cai
      -> Cai lai Python va TICK "Add Python to PATH".

  Vai video bao FAIL, so con lai ok
      -> Binh thuong. Chay lai run-30.bat: no BO QUA video da
         xong va chi lam lai cai con thieu.

  Dut giua buoi / tat may
      -> Chay lai run-30.bat, no tiep tuc cho do.

  Mang cong ty chan
      -> 3 trong 4 format can internet (lay du lieu gia). Format
         day mau nen thi KHONG can mang - font da nhung san.

Mot video loi KHONG lam chet ca batch: 29 cai con lai van chay.

hom nay khac hom qua.

doan - video ghi ro dieu do tren man hinh. Cac muc gia thi deu
